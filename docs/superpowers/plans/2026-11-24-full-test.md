## Review Focus

1. **BST vs GMT label** — trips in summer (e.g. 2027-07-10 09:00) are shown with a "GMT" suffix on Payment Successful / Receipt; a reader expects the right UK zone or no zone. → Task 3 step "BST label".
2. **Back button after paying** — browser Back from `/success` to `/notify` must not allow a second payment or show a broken page. → Task 3 step "back after pay".
3. **Session ends mid-checkout** — log out in a second tab, then press Next on Notification: expect a redirect to `/login`, not a silent failure. → Task 5 step "session ends".
4. **Long addresses** — a 200-character pickup must wrap, not overflow the 390 px frame, on Price Review, Notification, Receipt. → Task 3 step "long address".
5. **Phone edge input** — leading/trailing spaces, 16+ digits, `+` in the middle (`44+7700900123`). Expect: spaces trimmed → valid; 16 digits → format error; `+` in the middle → format error. → Task 3 step "phone edges".
